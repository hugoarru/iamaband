How it works

Three steps from a song you love to a band that plays it with you.

  1. Add a song

    Pick an MP3 from Files, or a song stored on your device from your music library.

  2. Let it separate

    The app splits it into four tracks: voice, bass, drums and the rest. It takes a few minutes, and keeps going while you do something else.

  3. Play along

    Turn down the part you play and take its place. Sing, play guitar, bass, keys or drums with the rest of the band.

Everything you need to practise

Made for musicians who learn by ear, singers who want a backing track, and anyone who has wanted to join their favourite band.

A fader for every track

Mute the voice for a backing track, pull everything down but the bass to hear its line, or bring the drums up to lock in with the beat.

Your key, your speed

Move the song up or down to fit your voice, and slow it down to work out a tricky passage. Key and speed change on their own.

Record your own track

Sing or play over the band and keep your take next to the other tracks. Pick your mic, nudge it into time, and redo it as often as you like.

Lyrics as it plays

Follow the words while you sing. The app writes them out from the voice track, on your device.

Take it with you

Export the mix just as you hear it, or one file per track, in M4A or WAV. Send it to your bandmates or open it in your music app.

iPhone, iPad and Mac

Practise with your phone on the music stand, your iPad on the piano, or your Mac in the studio.

Your songs stay on your device

Separating, lyrics and recording all happen on your iPhone, iPad or Mac. Nothing is uploaded. There's no account, no ads and no tracking.
